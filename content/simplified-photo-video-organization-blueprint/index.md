Managing a massive library of photos and videos can quickly turn into a chaotic mess if you do not have a strict, logical directory structure. Whether you are handling personal memories, client marketing assets, e-commerce product imagery, or educational materials, a context-based hierarchy keeps everything easily searchable.

This streamlined system organizes media by clear, high-level domains and granular subcategories using obvious, intuitive naming conventions.

---

## The Master Directory Blueprint

At the root level, maintain a single, dedicated entry point for all media assets. From there, branch out into clean subdirectories based on specific areas of your life and work.

### Root Folder: `Media Library`

#### 1. `01_Personal_Life`

Dedicated to your private life, social circles, and everyday documentation.

* **`Self`**: Personal portraits, milestone photos, and individual logs.
* **`Family`**: Immediate family gatherings, daily life, and household archives.
* **`Relatives`**: Extended family reunions and visits from distant relatives.
* **`Friends`**: Social events, dinners, outings, and friendly gatherings.
* **`Objects`**: Casual photos of items, possessions, or interesting things you encounter.

#### 2. `02_Agency_and_ECommerce`

Organized explicitly for digital agency operations, marketplace management, and commercial production assets.

* **`Marketing_and_Ads`**: Ad banners, promotional graphics, and campaign creative assets.
* **`Client_Meetings`**: Screen captures, consultation recordings, and briefing assets.
* **`Marketplace_Products`**: High-resolution catalog imagery and video showcases separated by platform:
* **`Wildberries`**
* **`Ozon`**



#### 3. `03_Education_and_Teaching`

Structured to house school-specific media, lesson materials, and academic events.

* **`School_Classes`**: Visual aids, student group photos, and active classroom session logs.
* **`School_Events`**: Ceremonies, presentations, holiday programs, and official school functions.

#### 4. `04_Geography_and_Travel`

Separates local everyday locations from major trips to make looking back at specific journeys effortless.

* **`Local_Visits`**: Cities, parks, and regional spots nearby.
* **`Travel_Trips`**: Distinct vacation folders organized by destination and year.

#### 5. `05_Storage_and_Unsorted`

A safety net for miscellaneous or temporary storage.

* **`Random_Captures`**: Quick snaps, temporary files, or memes that do not fit a specific category.
* **`Side_Projects`**: One-off personal experiments or test files.

---

## Summary & Conclusion

### Summary Checklist Matrix

| Top-Level Directory | Focus Area | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`01_Personal_Life`** | Relationships & Self | Family archives, friends, personal milestones |
| **`02_Agency_and_ECommerce`** | Agency & Commercial Work | E-commerce product photos, ad banners, client consultations |
| **`03_Education_and_Teaching`** | Academic & Teaching | Classroom sessions, school functions, student events |
| **`04_Geography_and_Travel`** | Locations | Local exploration and structured travel logs |
| **`05_Storage_and_Unsorted`** | Miscellaneous | Temporary file collection and side-project assets |

### Conclusion

The secret to maintaining a clean media drive is eliminating ambiguity. By sorting folders by context first (`Personal`, `Agency`, `Education`) rather than just relying on flat, chronological date dumps, you ensure that you can locate any specific file within three clicks. Use prefixes like `01_`, `02_` to force your file manager to keep your most important directories pinned to the top of the root tree permanently.
